    Classic Vanilla Sponge Cake

    Serves 8 | Prep: 15 min | Bake: 25-30 min at 180°C

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour (maida)
    • 1 tsp baking powder
    • 1/4 tsp salt
    • 3 eggs, room temperature
    • 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
    • 1/2 cup (115 g) butter, softened
    • 2 tbsp milk
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract

    Method

    1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease and line a 7-8 inch round tin.
    2. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together.
    3. Beat the butter and sugar for 3-4 minutes until pale and fluffy.
    4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each. Mix in the vanilla.
    5. Fold in the flour gently, adding the milk, until just combined.
    6. Pour into the tin and bake for 25-30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
    7. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then move to a wire rack.

    Chocolate Mug Cake

     Chocolate Mug Cake

    Serves 1 | Prep: 3 min | Microwave: 1-1.5 min

    Ingredients

    • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
    • 3 tbsp sugar
    • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
    • 1/4 tsp baking powder
    • 1 pinch of salt
    • 4 tbsp milk
    • 2 tbsp oil or melted butter
    • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
    • 1 tbsp chocolate chips (optional)

    Method

    1. In a large microwave-safe mug, mix all the dry ingredients.
    2. Add the milk, oil and vanilla. Stir until smooth, with no dry pockets at the bottom.
    3. Press the chocolate chips into the batter if using.
    4. Microwave on high for 60-90 seconds. The top should look set.
    5. Let it rest for 1 minute, then eat straight from the mug.

    Tip: Microwaves differ, so start with 60 seconds and add 10 seconds at a time. Overcooking makes it rubbery.

    Eggless Chocolate Cake

    Serves 8 | Prep: 15 min | Bake: 30-35 min at 180°C

    Ingredients

    • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
    • 1/3 cup (35 g) cocoa powder
    • 1 cup (200 g) sugar
    • 1 tsp baking soda
    • 1/2 tsp baking powder
    • 1/4 tsp salt
    • 1 cup (240 ml) milk
    • 1/2 cup (120 ml) oil
    • 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract

    Method

    1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease and line an 8 inch tin.
    2. Sift the flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder and salt into a bowl. Add the sugar.
    3. In another bowl, whisk the milk, oil, vinegar and vanilla.
    4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and whisk until smooth. Do not overmix.
    5. Pour into the tin and bake for 30-35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
    6. Cool completely before frosting or slicing.

    Why it works: The vinegar reacts with the baking soda to make the cake rise, replacing the eggs.

    Classic Banana Cake

    Serves 8 | Prep: 15 min | Bake: 45-50 min at 175°C

    Ingredients

    • 3 ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)
    • 1 3/4 cups (220 g) all-purpose flour
    • 1 tsp baking soda
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/2 cup (115 g) butter, melted
    • 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar
    • 2 eggs
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

    Method

    1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Grease and line a loaf tin.
    2. Mash the bananas well in a large bowl. Stir in the melted butter.
    3. Mix in the sugar, eggs and vanilla.
    4. Add the flour, baking soda and salt. Fold until just combined.
    5. Fold in the walnuts if using. Pour the batter into the tin.
    6. Bake for 45-50 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
    7. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes before turning out.

    Tip: The riper the bananas (brown spots), the sweeter and moister the cake.
